TARIFF:
HALDEN PARCEL CO.: DOMESTIC TARIFF (edition 9)

All prices are in cents. Apply the sections in order.

§1 Prohibited items. Lithium batteries, aerosols and perishable goods are not accepted for carriage.

§2 Weight. All weights are rounded UP to the next whole kilogram (minimum 1 kg) before any comparison or charge.

§3 Chargeable weight. PARCELS are charged on whichever is larger: actual weight, or volumetric weight (L x W x H in cm divided by 4000), each rounded up first. DOCUMENTS are never charged on volumetric weight.

§4 Base price. Zone A: 420 for the first kg, then 240 per further kg; Zone B: 850 for the first kg, then 250 per further kg; Zone C: 910 for the first kg, then 270 per further kg.

§5 Claims. Loss or damage must be reported within 14 days of the delivery date with photographs of the packaging.

§6 Surcharges. A weekend collection (Sat/Sun) adds 15% of the base price, rounded down; the percentage is never applied to other surcharges. A flat remote-area fee of 400 cents applies when the postcode starts with JU or GE; it is added after percentage surcharges.

§7 Discounts. GOLD accounts receive 15% off the total after all surcharges (rounded down to a whole cent). The GOLD discount is not available on Zone B consignments.

§8 Cap. A DOCUMENT never costs more than 1100 cents in total, after all surcharges and discounts.

§9 Delivery times. Zone A: next working day. Zone B: two working days. Zone C: three to five working days.
