TARIFF:
OSTREY EXPRESS: DOMESTIC TARIFF (edition 8)

All prices are in cents. Apply the sections in order.

§1 Claims. Loss or damage must be reported within 14 days of the delivery date with photographs of the packaging.

§2 Weight. All weights are rounded UP to the next whole kilogram (minimum 1 kg) before any comparison or charge.

§3 Chargeable weight. PARCELS are charged on whichever is larger: actual weight, or volumetric weight (L x W x H in cm divided by 4000), each rounded up first. DOCUMENTS are never charged on volumetric weight.

§4 Base price. Zone A: 400 for the first kg, then 140 per further kg; Zone B: 560 for the first kg, then 230 per further kg; Zone C: 800 for the first kg, then 250 per further kg.

§5 Delivery times. Zone A: next working day. Zone B: two working days. Zone C: three to five working days.

§6 Surcharges. A weekend collection (Sat/Sun) adds 25% of the base price, rounded down; the percentage is never applied to other surcharges. A flat remote-area fee of 200 cents applies when the postcode starts with ME or XG; it is added after percentage surcharges.

§7 Discounts. GOLD accounts receive 10% off the total after all surcharges (rounded down to a whole cent). Zone C consignments are excluded from the GOLD discount.

§8 Cap. The final price of any DOCUMENT is capped at 1000 cents.

§9 Tracking. Every consignment receives a tracking reference by email on the day of collection.
