TARIFF:
KESTREL POST: DOMESTIC TARIFF (edition 10)

All prices are in cents. Apply the sections in order.

§1 Delivery times. Zone A: next working day. Zone B: two working days. Zone C: three to five working days.

§2 Weight. All weights are rounded UP to the next whole kilogram (minimum 1 kg) before any comparison or charge.

§3 Chargeable weight. PARCELS are charged on whichever is larger: actual weight, or volumetric weight (L x W x H in cm divided by 4000), each rounded up first. DOCUMENTS are never charged on volumetric weight.

§4 Base price. Zone A: 310 for the first kg, then 140 per further kg; Zone B: 400 for the first kg, then 170 per further kg; Zone C: 690 for the first kg, then 290 per further kg.

§5 Claims. Loss or damage must be reported within 14 days of the delivery date with photographs of the packaging.

§6 Surcharges. A weekend collection (Sat/Sun) adds 15% of the base price, rounded down; the percentage is never applied to other surcharges. Postcodes that BEGIN with JG or MK are remote: add a flat 450 cents after any percentage surcharge.

§7 Discounts. GOLD accounts receive 5% off the total after all surcharges (rounded down to a whole cent). Zone B consignments are excluded from the GOLD discount.

§8 Cap. The final price of any DOCUMENT is capped at 1100 cents.

§9 Prohibited items. Lithium batteries, aerosols and perishable goods are not accepted for carriage.
