You are pricing consignments under the tariff below.

TARIFF:
NORTHVALE POST: DOMESTIC TARIFF (edition 6)

All prices are in cents. Apply the sections in order.

§1 Tracking. Every consignment receives a tracking reference by email on the day of collection.

§2 Weight. Weights are always rounded upward to a whole kilogram, never to the nearest; the minimum is 1 kg.

§3 Chargeable weight. For a PARCEL, the chargeable weight is the greater of its actual weight and its volumetric weight, where volumetric weight = length x width x height in cm / 5000. Each is rounded up separately before the two are compared. A DOCUMENT is always charged on its actual weight only.

§4 Base price. Zone A: 790 for the first kg, then 160 per further kg; Zone B: 820 for the first kg, then 220 per further kg; Zone C: 900 for the first kg, then 290 per further kg.

§5 Definitions. 'Consignment' means one item collected from one address. 'Working day' excludes public holidays.

§6 Surcharges. A weekend collection (Sat/Sun) adds 15% of the base price, rounded down; the percentage is never applied to other surcharges. Postcodes that BEGIN with BR or SZ are remote: add a flat 250 cents after any percentage surcharge.

§7 Discounts. GOLD accounts receive 10% off the total after all surcharges (rounded down to a whole cent). Zone C consignments are excluded from the GOLD discount.

§8 Prohibited items. Lithium batteries, aerosols and perishable goods are not accepted for carriage.
