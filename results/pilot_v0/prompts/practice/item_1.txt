TARIFF:
WEXCOMBE POST: DOMESTIC TARIFF (edition 5)

All prices are in cents. Apply the sections in order.

§1 Definitions. 'Consignment' means one item collected from one address. 'Working day' excludes public holidays.

§2 Weight. All weights are rounded UP to the next whole kilogram (minimum 1 kg) before any comparison or charge.

§3 Chargeable weight. PARCELS are charged on whichever is larger: actual weight, or volumetric weight (L x W x H in cm divided by 6000), each rounded up first. DOCUMENTS are never charged on volumetric weight.

§4 Base price. Zone A: 380 for the first kg, then 100 per further kg; Zone B: 710 for the first kg, then 190 per further kg; Zone C: 830 for the first kg, then 200 per further kg.

§5 Prohibited items. Lithium batteries, aerosols and perishable goods are not accepted for carriage.

§6 Surcharges. Collections on Saturday or Sunday attract a surcharge of 25% of the base price only (rounded down to a whole cent). A flat remote-area fee of 250 cents applies when the postcode starts with TY or KS; it is added after percentage surcharges.

§7 Discounts. GOLD accounts receive 15% off the total after all surcharges (rounded down to a whole cent). Zone B consignments are excluded from the GOLD discount.

§8 Claims. Loss or damage must be reported within 14 days of the delivery date with photographs of the packaging.
