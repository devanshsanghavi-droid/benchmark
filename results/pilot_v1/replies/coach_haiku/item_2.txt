<note>
Key points for accurate pricing:

§3 Reclassification: A DOCUMENT weighing more than 1 kg (after rounding) becomes a PARCEL for all pricing purposes.

§6 Heavy items: The heavy surcharge (60¢) applies to kg 7 and above—these kg use the modified per-kg rate (base rate + 60¢). Build your base price correctly with this structure, not as a separate addition.

§8 Surcharges: Weekend is 20% of base price. Remote areas (postcodes starting JL or JX) add a flat 300¢ AFTER percentage surcharges.

§9 Discounts: GOLD discount (15% of base + percentage surcharges) does NOT apply to Zone A consignments—check this carefully! Flat fees are never discounted.

Order matters: base price → weekend surcharge → remote fee → GOLD discount.
</note>
