<note>
Your one systematic error: you treated the §6 heavy-kg increase (+60 per kg above 6) as a separate flat surcharge. It is not. It raises the per-kg rate, so it is part of the base price. Build the full base first (ordinary rate up to 6 kg, ordinary+60 for every kg beyond 6), then apply the weekend 20% and the GOLD 15% (round each down) to that whole figure. Only the remote fee (300) is flat: add it last, undiscounted.

Examples: Zone C, chargeable 19 kg, GOLD, JL postcode: base = 840 + 5x260 + 13x320 = 6300; discount 945; 5355 + 300 = 5655 (not 5772). Zone A, 27 kg, Sunday: base 4590 + 20% (918) = 5508 (not 5256).

Your volumetric, reclassification and Zone A exclusion were right; keep them.
</note>
