You are pricing consignments under the tariff below.

TARIFF:
TAMSIN EXPRESS: DOMESTIC TARIFF (edition 8)

All prices are in cents. Apply the sections in order.

§1 Definitions. 'Consignment' means one item collected from one address. 'Working day' excludes public holidays.

§2 Weight. All weights are rounded UP to the next whole kilogram (minimum 1 kg) before any comparison or charge.

§3 Reclassification. Any DOCUMENT weighing more than 2 kg (after rounding) is reclassified as a PARCEL and priced as one in every section.

§4 Chargeable weight. PARCELS are charged on whichever is larger: actual weight, or volumetric weight (L x W x H in cm divided by 6000), each rounded up first. DOCUMENTS are never charged on volumetric weight.

§5 Base price. Zone A: 340 for the first kg, then 120 per further kg; Zone B: 540 for the first kg, then 180 per further kg; Zone C: 600 for the first kg, then 280 per further kg.

§6 Heavy items. For each chargeable kg above 5 kg, the per-kg rate is increased by 60 cents. The first 5 kg are charged at the ordinary rates.

§7 Tracking. Every consignment receives a tracking reference by email on the day of collection.

§8 Surcharges. A weekend collection (Sat/Sun) adds 10% of the base price, rounded down; the percentage is never applied to other surcharges. A flat remote-area fee of 300 cents applies when the postcode starts with XU or PG; it is added after percentage surcharges.

§9 Discounts. GOLD accounts receive 15% off the base price plus any percentage surcharge (rounded down to a whole cent); flat fees are never discounted. Zone C consignments are excluded from the GOLD discount.

§10 Cap. A DOCUMENT never costs more than 1200 cents in total, after all surcharges and discounts.

§11 Claims. Loss or damage must be reported within 14 days of the delivery date with photographs of the packaging.
