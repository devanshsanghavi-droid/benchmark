You are pricing consignments under the tariff below.

TARIFF:
HALDEN COURIERS: DOMESTIC TARIFF (edition 9)

All prices are in cents. Apply the sections in order.

§1 Delivery times. Zone A: next working day. Zone B: two working days. Zone C: three to five working days.

§2 Weight. Weights are always rounded upward to a whole kilogram, never to the nearest; the minimum is 1 kg.

§3 Reclassification. A DOCUMENT whose rounded actual weight exceeds 1 kg is treated as a PARCEL for all purposes in this tariff.

§4 Chargeable weight. For a PARCEL, the chargeable weight is the greater of its actual weight and its volumetric weight, where volumetric weight = length x width x height in cm / 4000. Each is rounded up separately before the two are compared. A DOCUMENT is always charged on its actual weight only.

§5 Base price. Zone A: 470 for the first kg, then 110 per further kg; Zone B: 560 for the first kg, then 240 per further kg; Zone C: 840 for the first kg, then 260 per further kg.

§6 Heavy items. For each chargeable kg above 6 kg, the per-kg rate is increased by 60 cents. The first 6 kg are charged at the ordinary rates.

§7 Definitions. 'Consignment' means one item collected from one address. 'Working day' excludes public holidays.

§8 Surcharges. A weekend collection (Sat/Sun) adds 20% of the base price, rounded down; the percentage is never applied to other surcharges. Postcodes that BEGIN with JL or JX are remote: add a flat 300 cents after any percentage surcharge.

§9 Discounts. GOLD accounts receive 15% off the base price plus any percentage surcharge (rounded down to a whole cent); flat fees are never discounted. The GOLD discount is not available on Zone A consignments.

§10 Cap. A DOCUMENT never costs more than 800 cents in total, after all surcharges and discounts.

§11 Tracking. Every consignment receives a tracking reference by email on the day of collection.
