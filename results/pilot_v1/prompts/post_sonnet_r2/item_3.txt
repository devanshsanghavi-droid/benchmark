You are pricing consignments under the tariff below.

TARIFF:
HALDEN PARCEL CO.: DOMESTIC TARIFF (edition 10)

All prices are in cents. Apply the sections in order.

§1 Delivery times. Zone A: next working day. Zone B: two working days. Zone C: three to five working days.

§2 Weight. Weights are always rounded upward to a whole kilogram, never to the nearest; the minimum is 1 kg.

§3 Reclassification. A DOCUMENT whose rounded actual weight exceeds 1 kg is treated as a PARCEL for all purposes in this tariff.

§4 Chargeable weight. For a PARCEL, the chargeable weight is the greater of its actual weight and its volumetric weight, where volumetric weight = length x width x height in cm / 4000. Each is rounded up separately before the two are compared. A DOCUMENT is always charged on its actual weight only.

§5 Base price. Zone A: 530 for the first kg, then 150 per further kg; Zone B: 660 for the first kg, then 260 per further kg; Zone C: 800 for the first kg, then 280 per further kg.

§6 Heavy items. For each chargeable kg above 8 kg, the per-kg rate is increased by 80 cents. The first 8 kg are charged at the ordinary rates.

§7 Claims. Loss or damage must be reported within 14 days of the delivery date with photographs of the packaging.

§8 Surcharges. A weekend collection (Sat/Sun) adds 25% of the base price, rounded down; the percentage is never applied to other surcharges. Postcodes that BEGIN with GT or XL are remote: add a flat 400 cents after any percentage surcharge.

§9 Discounts. For GOLD accounts, 10% (rounded down) is deducted from the base price and percentage surcharges only. Flat fees such as the remote-area or oversize fee are charged in full. Zone A consignments are excluded from the GOLD discount.

§10 Cap. A DOCUMENT never costs more than 800 cents in total, after all surcharges and discounts.

§11 Prohibited items. Lithium batteries, aerosols and perishable goods are not accepted for carriage.

<<<
Your one systematic error: you treated the §6 heavy-kg increase (+80 per kg above 8) as a separate flat surcharge. It is not. It raises the per-kg rate, so it is part of the base price. Build the full base first (ordinary rate up to 8 kg, ordinary+80 for every kg beyond 8), then apply the weekend 25% and the GOLD 10% (round each down) to that whole figure. Only the remote fee (400) is flat: add it last, undiscounted. Example: Zone C, chargeable 11 kg, Sunday, GOLD, XL postcode. Base = 800 + 7x280 + 3x360 = 3840. Weekend +960 = 4800. GOLD -480 = 4320. Remote +400 = 4720 (not 4690). Your weights, volumetric, document reclassification and caps were right; keep them.
